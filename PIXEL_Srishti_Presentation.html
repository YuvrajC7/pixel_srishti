Agentic Geospatial Assistant for Watershed Monitoring

Team: JALDI THE LATE

Smart India Hackathon 2026 - Review 1

The Core Problem

  • Data Silos: Satellites capture optical, SAR, and multispectral data, but they are processed in isolation.
  • High Barrier to Entry: Analyzing GeoTIFFs requires complex desktop GIS software and highly specialized training.
  • Operational Bottleneck: Field officers cannot easily query data to track critical metrics like vegetation recovery or water body expansion, forcing reliance on manual field surveys.

Our Solution

A multimodal AI interface that democratizes spatial analysis.

  • Natural Language Querying: Administrators ask plain-English questions ("Show me drainage changes since last year").
  • Agentic Orchestration: The system automatically selects the correct specialist model (Change Detection, Segmentation) to answer the query.
  • Interactive Mapping: Returns highly precise thematic GeoJSON masks rendered directly on a web-based Leaflet dashboard.

Technology Stack

Frontend & Backend

  • React.js & Tailwind CSS
  • React-Leaflet (Mapping)
  • Python / FastAPI
  • LangGraph (Orchestrator)

Geospatial & ML

  • Rasterio & GeoPandas
  • LLaVA / Grounding DINO
  • U-Net (Segmentation)
  • PyTorch & Transformers

Impact & Scalability

  • Reduces weeks of manual GIS processing down to instant automated insights.
  • Fundamentally eliminates the GIS training bottleneck for rural district administrators.
  • Highly scalable beyond watersheds: easily adaptable for crop-yield estimation, flood mapping, and urban sprawl tracking.
